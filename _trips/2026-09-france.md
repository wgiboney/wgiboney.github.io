## Day 1 – Paris On our own

 

We landed super early in the morning and explored a bit of Paris before we could check into our hotel.  We walked to the Arc De Triomphe.  It is a large monument built by Napoleon to celebrate and honor fallen soldiers.  It is a massive round about with about 6 lanes of traffic moving around it.  Really cool to see on day 1.  We wandered a bit and crashed pretty early because of the jet lag.  We met the rest of our tour group later that night and got settled into our hotel.  It’s a small hotel and the rooms are even smaller!  Just ask Bethany, but really nice.
